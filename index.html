The problem

Every app only knows its own shelf.

Open any streaming app and it recommends from what it sells. It has no idea what you watched somewhere else, and it will never point you to a better pick on a different service.

Watchwise looks across all of them. One taste profile, stored on your computer, picks from the platforms you chose, in your country.

How it works

Three steps, no account.

  1. Tell it your taste

    A short onboarding: your country, your platforms, the genres you like. Watch history is optional and import is coming soon.

  2. It builds one local profile

    Watchwise pulls a real catalog from TMDB and checks which of your platforms stream each title in your country. Your profile is saved in a local database, and scheduled jobs keep the catalog fresh while the app is open.

  3. Pick, watch, mark watched

    Choose from rows made for you. Mark a title as watched and it disappears from your picks and nudges your taste.

Automatic updates and alerts

It keeps itself fresh

Scheduled jobs run while Watchwise is open and catch up after a break: they find trending titles, new releases and titles similar to ones you liked, re-check where titles stream, update your taste, tidy the database, and make a weekly backup and health check.

A Schedules panel shows each job’s last and next run, and lets you pause or run it. A bell alerts you to new titles, and when a saved title arrives on, or leaves, one of your apps.

Watched already

Mark it once, it learns

“Watched already” saves the title to a local database, hides it from your picks and teaches your taste. Taste also learns from how you use the app on your computer (titles you open, trailers you play, “Watch on” clicks), with recent activity counting more.

Privacy

Your taste stays home.

Stays on your computer

  • Your taste profile and what you marked as watched, in a local database
  • Usage signals that teach your taste (opens, trailer plays, “Watch on” clicks), stored only on your computer and clearable any time from Schedules
  • Your lists and settings
  • No account, no login, no cloud. This site has no analytics or trackers
  • The app’s API listens only on your own machine (127.0.0.1)

Sent to TMDB

  • Your country, to find where titles stream
  • The platforms and genres you chose, to fetch a matching catalog
  • Never your watch history or usage signals
