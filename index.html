Every app only knows its own shelf.

Netflix will never tell you the better pick is on Prime Video. Each app recommends what it sells, and none of them has seen what you watched somewhere else.

One profile, built where you can see it.

  1. A short onboarding

    Your country, the platforms you have, which AI tool you use, your taste, and optionally some watch history. History import from other services is coming soon; for now you can add titles by hand.

  2. A profile in a local database

    Watchwise pulls a real catalog from TMDB and marks which of your platforms stream each title in your country. Your profile lives in a SQLite file on your machine.

  3. Pick, watch, mark it watched

    Rows are built from your taste. “Watched already” hides a title and teaches the profile. So do the titles you open, the trailers you play and the Watch on buttons you click.

Every pick says why.

The title page shows a match score with the reason in plain words, such as “Because you like Action, Sci-Fi and Crime”. Below it: where to watch on your platforms, cast, details and similar titles.

Today the score is a simple genre-and-history score. The AI engine is coming soon.

Leave it idle and it tells you stories.

After a few idle minutes the lights dim and Intermission takes over: stories, numbers, cast and moments from titles, built from verified TMDB data.

It keeps itself fresh.

Jobs run while Watchwise is open and catch up when you come back after a break.

Find new content
Trending titles, new releases and titles similar to ones you liked.
Check where titles stream
Alerts you when a saved title arrives on, or leaves, one of your apps.
Update your taste
Recalculates from what you watched, rated and opened. Recent activity counts more.
Tidy, back up, check health
Daily cleanup, a weekly backup and a weekly health check.

Open Schedules to see each job’s last and next run, pause it or run it now. The bell lists new titles and streaming changes.

The same app on your phone.

Open your computer’s address on port 5173 from a phone on the same Wi-Fi. The layout switches to a tab bar. Your data stays on the computer.

The 44-second version.

No sound. It downloads only when you press play.

Hand this to your AI.

Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Gemini CLI. It installs everything and asks you for one thing: a free TMDB token.

Clone https://github.com/sumitgangwar127/Watchwise, read AGENTS.md, then install it and start it. When you need my TMDB token, ask me for it (it's free at themoviedb.org/settings/api) and put it in .env. Then open http://localhost:5173 and tell me when it's ready.

What happens next: about a minute while it installs, then Watchwise opens in your browser and starts onboarding.

Or do it by hand

git clone https://github.com/sumitgangwar127/Watchwise.git
cd Watchwise
npm install
npm start

Before npm start, copy .env.example to .env and add your free TMDB token as TMDB_ACCESS_TOKEN. Needs Node 22.9 or newer. Then open http://localhost:5173. Without a token the app runs on sample titles.

Your taste stays on your computer.

Stays here

  • Your taste profile and what you marked as watched, in a local database
  • Usage signals that teach your taste (titles opened, trailers played, Watch on clicks), clearable any time from Schedules
  • Your lists and settings
  • The app’s API listens only on 127.0.0.1. No account, no analytics here or on this site.

Goes to TMDB

  • Your country, to find where titles stream
  • The platforms and genres you chose, to fetch a matching catalog
  • Never your watch history or usage signals
